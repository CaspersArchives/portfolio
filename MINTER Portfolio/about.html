Who am I?

My name is Reg Tolentino, or you can call me by my alias, Casper. I'm an artist from the Philippines. Bborn and raised in Saudi Arabia in 2003, 'til moving to the Philippines in 2017. Currently, I'm still a college. From as early as seven, I founnd myself in the world of Films and Music, and it evolved into a love and facination, maybe obsession, with some of those interests coming to fruition in mid 2017 to early 2018, with my first attempt at music production, and filmmaking later in 2024. I'm still trying to hone my craft and figure out what my creative identity would be. In some aspects I have, it may not be as in depth, but I have one at the least. But as of now, I'm still experimenting, finding inspiration wherever, playing around with sounds, styles, and techniques, doing whatever I please, and what others want from me, and maybe the puzzle pieces will al come together.
